PowerStudio is a power system analysis workbench. Draw a network, then run load flow, short-circuit, N-1 contingency and stability studies on it. Everything runs on your device: no account, no server, no tracking.

A workspace built for the job

Familiar layout, made for the browser

  • Ribbon, model tree, diagram and inspector. Every element's data is grouped by the study it feeds, with units and limits.
  • Edit on the diagram. Snap busbars into line with guides, align and distribute them, shape routes that stay square or route them around obstacles, and find your way with an overview map.
  • Readable results. Names and result boxes are placed clear of each other, with a leader where one has to move away; drag any of them where you want it.
  • Results where you look. Loading and voltage colours, result boxes, sortable tables, CSV export, and results marked stale after an edit.
  • Fast to drive. A command palette, keyboard shortcuts for every study, undo and redo for every change, light and dark themes.
  • Projects. Study cases, scenarios and variants, a run log with the hashes of every result, and a spreadsheet for every element class.
  • Your files. Open CGMES, PSS/E RAW with DYR, and MATPOWER files; export projects (encrypted if you like), the operating point as CGMES, a study report, SVG, PNG and CSV.

Local-first

Your network never leaves your machine

No account, no server

Networks save to your browser's storage as you work. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing is uploaded.

Network access is switched off

The app's Content-Security-Policy sets connect-src 'none', so the page cannot send data anywhere, by design and by test.

One file, offline

The whole app is a single {{appSize}} HTML file. Download it, open it from disk, and it runs without a connection.

Scope

What it is, and what it is not

PowerStudio is a capable study tool, not a validated engineering product. Check results that matter with a validated tool.

  • Balanced networks. Positive-sequence load flow; no unbalanced three-phase studies.
  • Short circuit not certified. It follows IEC 60909-0 and meets the TR 60909-4 example, but it is not certified, and the steady-state current and DC component are not computed.
  • Stability, first wave. No salient-pole machines and no renewable, HVDC or SVC dynamics; no electromagnetic transients.
  • Contingencies by hand. N-1 is automatic; multi-element contingencies are listed, not enumerated, and there is no optimal power flow.
  • Diagrams of large networks are dense. Networks of tens of thousands of busbars open and solve, but there are no substation diagrams yet.
  • Tested on macOS. Chromium (WebGPU and Canvas 2D), Firefox and WebKit pass every test; Safari itself and Windows were not tried.

The full list is in the Scope and boundaries section of the README.
